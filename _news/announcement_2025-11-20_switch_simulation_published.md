Our paper “[Simulating the quantum switch with quantum circuits is computationally hard](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.18202)” was just published in [Nature Communications](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64996-6)! It brings together the results of our two earlier preprints on quantum-switch simulation. This was a collaboration with the University of Tokyo.
